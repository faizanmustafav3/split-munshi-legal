Delete your Split Munshi account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Split Munshi account and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Split Munshi app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (or Account Settings).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete My Account.

You will be signed out and your account is scheduled for deletion.

Can't open the app?

Email REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-SUPPORT-EMAIL from the address registered on your account, with the subject “Delete my Split Munshi account”. We will start the same 30-day deletion process for you.

The 30-day grace period

Deletion is not immediate. For 30 days your account stays recoverable: sign back in during that time and you will be offered the option to restore it. After 30 days it is permanently deleted as described below.

Data that is deleted

  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your password and your ability to sign in
  • Your push-notification device tokens

Data that is kept

  • Expenses, splits, settlements, group memberships and activity history that involve you. Other people's balances depend on these records, so they cannot be removed without corrupting their accounts.
  • Your display name, which remains on those shared records with “(Deleted)” added after it. It is no longer linked to any email, phone number or login.

Retention

The only retention period is the 30-day grace period above. The kept records listed above remain for as long as the groups they belong to exist.
